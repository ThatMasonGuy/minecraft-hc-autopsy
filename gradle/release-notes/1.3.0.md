HC Autopsy 1.3.0 adds automatic post-wipe stat leaderboards after Hardcore
run summaries.

## Highlights

- After stat capture finishes, HC Autopsy now sends a prettified run
  leaderboard in game chat after the wipe summary.
- Discord wipe notifications now send a second leaderboard embed after the
  main wipe embed.
- The automatic leaderboard includes:
  - Most time played
  - Most blocks broken
  - Most damage taken
  - Most damage dealt
  - Most diamonds mined, counting both regular and deepslate diamond ore
